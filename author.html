From Kharkiv to Switzerland. A personal journey through the world of Whisky.

Oleksandr Martynov

Oleksandr Martynov

OVER 2,000 WHISKIES. YEARS OF DISCOVERY.

Since 2013, Oleksandr Martynov has dedicated himself to the exploration, tasting and study of Whisky.

His experience goes far beyond collecting bottles. Over the years, he has personally tasted and studied over 2,000 different Whiskies, often returning to the same expressions over months or even years to explore their character, complexity and evolution.

For him, Whisky tasting is not about finishing a bottle in one sitting. It is a process of careful observation, repeated tasting and comparative analysis — understanding what makes each Whisky distinctive and how it relates to others.

BEYOND BORDERS. BEYOND CATEGORIES.

A Scottish single malt can reveal entirely new dimensions when compared with an Irish, Indian or Japanese Whisky. Sometimes the most fascinating discoveries emerge when single malts are placed alongside carefully selected blends.

Oleksandr's approach goes beyond geographical boundaries and traditional classifications. Each tasting is an opportunity to explore similarities, contrasts, production philosophies, flavour profiles and the influence of time.

THE LOST ART OF GREAT BLENDS

One of his particular areas of expertise is the study of historic Scotch and Irish blends from the 1950s to the 1990s.

These Whiskies offer a fascinating perspective on a period when some premium blends incorporated exceptionally high proportions of malt Whisky — in certain cases, approximately 60–80%.

Built around outstanding malt components, some of these historic blends possess remarkable depth, texture and exceptionally long finishes. In comparative tastings, they can rival single malts and, in Oleksandr's experience, surpass many contemporary blends and even some modern single malts.

Their stories reveal a side of Whisky history that is often overlooked: great blending was not simply an art of combining spirits. It was an art of selecting and bringing together exceptional Whiskies.

KNOWLEDGE THROUGH EXPERIENCE

Today, Oleksandr Martynov shares the knowledge gained through years of tasting, research and comparative study.

Through OM Whisky Academy, every tasting becomes more than an introduction to a particular bottle. It is an opportunity to understand the Whisky itself, discover its historical context and explore the people, decisions and traditions behind it.
