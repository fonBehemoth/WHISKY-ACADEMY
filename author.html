Meet the founder

Oleksandr Martynov

Oleksandr started by collecting whiskies from around the world. His curiosity took him deeper into the history of brands, the craft of distilling, and the character of individual releases.

Over time, that personal journey became something to share. Through tastings and conversations, he introduces both newcomers and enthusiasts to the wider world of whisky.

Whisky Academy grew from a wish to share knowledge, experience and the pleasure of discovery.
Discover the collection

Oleksandr Martynov has been collecting whisky since 2013. Today, his private collection holds 2,000 whiskies. His interests span the history of brands, distilling, blending and the character of individual releases.
