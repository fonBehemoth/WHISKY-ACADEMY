## Промпт

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE refined contemporary simplification of the ornate interwoven monogram in the supplied reference, as a polished visual concept for an independent Whisky academy. Keep the core idea of a capital M interlaced with a circular O-like letterform, but completely redraw it into a calm, elegant, modern custom monogram. Flat single muted champagne-gold (#C7AD73) artwork on a completely smooth deep charcoal (#141615) background. Sharp clean vector-like edges, front-on, no embossing, no bevels, no metallic texture, no glitter, no gradients, no shadows, no paper grain. One centred monogram with generous margins in a square canvas; visually balanced and usable at small website-header sizes. Do not include any words, labels, studio credit, watermark or the reference's UNFOLD STUDIO inscription. Deliver a new modern identity concept inspired by the reference's interwoven structure, rather than a trace of its ornamental details.
