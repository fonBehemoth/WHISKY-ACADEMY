## Промпт

Use case: logo-brand. Refine the supplied ornate monogram into a contemporary, elegant custom-lettered identity while retaining its distinctive personality. The client rejected an earlier generic straight serif M inside a perfect circle as primitive: DO NOT do that. Work closely from THIS original reference only. Preserve the fluid bowed stems, sweeping diagonal movement, asymmetric calligraphic rhythm, layered interlacing and sophisticated high-contrast curves of the underlying letterforms. Preserve the relationship of the large curved M-like strokes and the surrounding O-like oval; do not straighten them into ordinary typeset lettering, do not replace the structure with a geometric ring. Simplify the ORNAMENT, not the underlying letter architecture. Remove little flowers, leaf/frond motifs, tiny spirals, decorative bumps and redundant curly endings. Keep two restrained long sweeping terminals, gracefully tapered, with clean controlled ends instead of tight ornamental curls. Keep about three carefully designed over-under crossings with precise negative-space separations, so this is still a crafted, intricate and distinctive monogram. IMPORTANT CLIENT REQUIREMENT: KEEP THE FINE ENGRAVED HATCHING from the reference. Use neatly spaced, delicate diagonal parallel hatch lines inside selected broad curved strokes of the surrounding letter, matching stroke direction and curvature. Keep contrasting smooth ribbon-like strokes in the central letter. Hatching should feel like precision engraving, never a dense scribble; let it breathe. Refine excess double/triple borders into a single crisp outline plus deliberate engraved panels. Contemporary elegance through excellent curves, balanced counterspaces, controlled stroke contrast and restrained ornament. Warm muted champagne-gold on deep charcoal, subtle satin-foil sheen and very fine quiet dark paper texture. No heavy bevels, no deep cast shadows, no glitter, no crown, no crest, no whisky glass icon. One centered monogram, generous margins, front view, square presentation. No words, no caption, no watermark, no UNFOLD STUDIO credit. This must feel like the original monogram thoughtfully edited by an expert lettering designer, NOT reduced to an elementary M and circle.
