Our philosophy

More Than a Tasting. A Deeper Understanding.

Whisky is more than a drink. Behind every bottle lies a story of craftsmanship, geography, history, commerce and human ambition.

At OM Whisky Academy, we explore these connections through historical research, comparative tasting and the study of remarkable Whisky collections.

Our approach goes beyond tasting notes. We seek to understand what makes each Whisky distinctive — and why.

The collection

A Personal Collection. A World of Knowledge.

Over 2,000 Whiskies from Oleksandr Martynov’s personal collection — acquired, tasted, studied and compared over more than a decade.

From historic bottlings to contemporary releases, the collection offers an opportunity to explore the evolution of Whisky, the character of distilleries and the differences between generations of production.

Oleksandr Martynov

The founder

A Collector’s Passion. A Shared Discovery.

Oleksandr Martynov’s journey into the world of Whisky began with collecting bottles from distilleries and producers around the world. What started as a personal passion developed into years of comparative tasting, historical research and the study of Whisky craftsmanship.

Since 2013, he has explored the stories behind individual releases, distilleries and the people who shaped the industry.

Today, through OM Whisky Academy, he shares this experience with others — turning individual discoveries into a shared journey.

Whisky & community

Bringing People Together Through Whisky.

In 2023, a Whisky enthusiasts’ club within the Rotary community in Kharkiv inspired the idea behind OM Whisky Academy.

The initiative grew into a wider community, with Whisky clubs in Kyiv, Kharkiv and Dnipro.
